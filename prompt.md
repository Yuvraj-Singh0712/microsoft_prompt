## ⚡ Ready-to-submit Prompt

**Act as an expert college canteen manager and business planner.**

You have a **budget of ₹10,000 to improve a college canteen for one week**. Design a practical plan that keeps food **affordable, tasty, hygienic, and profitable** while handling unpredictable student demand.

Create a detailed plan covering:

* 🍕 **Menu:** Select 6–8 popular food and drink items suitable for college students.
* 💰 **Pricing:** Set affordable selling prices and estimate the profit margin for each item.
* 📦 **Quantities:** Decide how many units/portions to prepare for each item per day.
* 🧾 **₹10,000 Budget:** Give a clear breakdown of how the entire budget will be spent.
* 📊 **Demand Management:** Explain how to handle high-demand periods, sold-out items, low-demand items, and unexpected changes in demand.
* ♻️ **Waste Control:** Suggest ways to reduce food wastage and manage leftover ingredients.
* 👥 **Student Satisfaction:** Include strategies for quick service, variety, hygiene, and affordability.

**Important constraints:**

* Do not exceed ₹10,000 in initial spending.
* Keep prices realistic for college students.
* Show calculations clearly.
* Make reasonable assumptions about the number of students served and state those assumptions.
* Aim for a sustainable one-week operation rather than spending the entire budget on a single day.

**Final output:** Present the solution as a **7-day plan with a menu table, prices, quantities, daily costs, expected revenue/profit, total budget allocation, and demand-management strategy.**

**Goal:** Create the most practical and financially sustainable one-week canteen plan using ₹10,000.
